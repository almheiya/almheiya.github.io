For Frenz

Dear Frenz,

I wanted to write this little letter for you because sometimes there are things that are easier to say when they are written down.

Thank you for being someone who has been part of my life and for all the memories we have shared. Even the simple moments can become memories that we remember for a long time.

I hope you know that you are appreciated more than you might realize. I am grateful for the conversations, the laughs, and all the little moments in between.

Whatever happens in the future, I hope you continue growing, learning, and becoming the person you want to be.

Thank you for being you.

With love,
Mia
