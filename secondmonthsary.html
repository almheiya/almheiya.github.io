My Dearest,

If someone ever asked me to define what home truly means, I wouldn’t point to a street name, a doorway, or four walls. I would point straight to you—to the quiet comfort of your laugh, the warmth of your hand in mine, and every ordinary afternoon that somehow became my favorite memory.

Looking back at all the snapshots woven into the heart above, I realize how effortlessly you turned fleeting moments into the safest place I have ever known. No matter how loud or hurried the world outside becomes, being beside you always feels like coming back to stillness.

Thank you for being my sanctuary, my favorite story, and the person I always want to come home to—today, tomorrow, and in every season still waiting for us.

Forever & always yours,

With all my love
