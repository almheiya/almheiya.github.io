A sunny day in May
It’s been raining straight the past few days, but on one of the nicest days in May, I met up with my friends and we went to the sand hills. We took lunch and found a quiet spot in the shade that felt so peaceful and green.
I’d definitely go back. We climbed trees and took group photos on the digicam.
There were so many funny attempts before we finally got one. Such a lovely day—hoping for more warm weather like this soon.
